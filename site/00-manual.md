# MSP Atlas manual

This manual shows you how to use MSP Atlas day to day: finding a client's documentation, keeping passwords safe, watching the health of the devices you look after, and running Atlas itself. It covers version {{version}}.

> [!note] How to read this manual
> Menu paths are written in bold with arrows, for example **Admin → People & access**. Atlas has three menu styles (chapter 2 shows them), and every page is in the same place in each. Numbered steps are things to do in order. What you can see depends on your role, so some pages in this manual may not be in your menu.
