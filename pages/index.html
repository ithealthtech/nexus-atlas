Documentation that stays current

Assets built from layouts, runbooks with checklists and review dates, and version history with line-by-line comparison and restore.

02

A vault with a record

Every reveal, copy, change, and share is recorded. Restrict entries to named people, and require a reason for each reveal.

03

Clients kept apart

Per-client access levels and groups. A client you can't access looks exactly like one that doesn't exist.

04

Bring your data

Import from Hudu or CSV, sync devices and tickets from the ConnectWise platform, export any client as a zip, and automate with a REST API and scoped API keys.

New in 1.7

A dashboard that runs your day, and a vault that goes where you work

The 1.7 releases add ConnectWise platform sync, ticket and expiry dashboards, automated password rotation, and apps for the browser and Windows.

Customizable dashboard

Show, hide, and reorder cards, and star clients, documents, and assets as Favorites. Client pages open with quick notes, primary contact, hours, and maintenance window.

ConnectWise platform sync

Devices sync from ConnectWise RMM as device assets with manufacturer, last check-in, online status from the heartbeat, and endpoint protection, feeding the RMM health charts.

Ticket dashboard

Tickets from the ConnectWise platform with opened and closed per day, a tile per status, and a ticket table on each client, each linked to its page in ConnectWise.

Asset statistics

Servers, workstations, switches, network devices, printers, and phones at a glance, with an operating system chart that marks the ones out of support.

Domain and SSL tracking

Domains are re-checked with their registry and certificates read from each site on a schedule, so expirations and alerts stay accurate. Each client has its own trackers.

Warranty lookup

Dell, Lenovo, and HP warranty end dates are filled in from the serial number, with no vendor API keys to set up. Dates you typed in are never replaced.

Automated password rotation

Local administrator and service account passwords change on a schedule through ConnectWise RMM. The vault updates only after the device confirms.

Browser extension and Windows app

Fill saved logins in Edge and Chrome, or press Ctrl+Shift+Space in the Atlas for Windows tray app (preview). Neither stores passwords, and every fill or copy is recorded.

Vault policies and emergency access

Set generator rules, require reveal reasons everywhere, name trusted administrators for emergency access, stream logs to a SIEM, and keep secure notes, encrypted files, and Send links.

Product tour

Everything a technician needs for a client, in one place

Each client has its own assets, documents, passwords, contacts, locations, and activity. Ctrl+K search finds any of them — including by IP address or serial number — and returns only what you're allowed to see.

How it works

One service, one database, one key you keep

  1. Host and origin checksRequests must match your public address; cross-site requests are refused.
  2. A real sessionHttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies, plus a CSRF token on every change.
  3. Two-step sign-inRequired for staff: an authenticator app or a passkey.
  4. Per-client accessThe highest of your baseline, grants, and groups, capped by your role.
  5. Passwords need moreEdit-and-passwords access, the restriction list, and a reason when the client requires one. Every reveal is recorded.

Roadmap

What's coming next

Planned work, not yet released. The ConnectWise items reuse the existing ConnectWise platform connection, with no new vendor keys. Some depend on which ConnectWise products you license.

Planned

Device inventory from ConnectWise

  • Installed software and versions on each device, tied to license records
  • Who signs in to each device, linked to the Atlas contact
  • Hyper-V and VMware hosts related to their virtual machines
  • Disk, memory, and CPU trends with low-disk warnings
  • RMM device groups and the effective policy for each device
Planned

Security and compliance

  • Backup status and DR readiness on the client overview
  • OS and third-party patch compliance, with missing patches per device
  • Vulnerability counts by severity per device and client
  • Open ConnectWise MDR security cases and their impacted devices
Planned

ConnectWise write-back

  • An “Open in Atlas” link on devices and companies inside ConnectWise
  • Ticket notes when a password or document is used for a ticket, and tickets for upcoming expirations
Planned

Contacts and sites

  • Client contacts synced from ConnectWise into Atlas contacts
  • ConnectWise sites imported as Atlas locations, with devices filed under them
Planned

Signed Windows app

  • A signed MSIX installer and automatic updates for Atlas for Windows, waiting on a code-signing certificate

Before you store client credentials

Know the current boundary.

Version 1.7.6 covers documentation, the vault with secure notes, encrypted files, and Send, vault policies with emergency access and SIEM streaming, accounts and security, sign-in with Microsoft Entra ID, the REST API, Hudu and CSV import, ConnectWise RMM device sync and tickets from the ConnectWise platform, Microsoft 365 sync, keyless warranty lookup for Dell, Lenovo, and HP, domain and SSL tracking, automated password rotation, a browser extension for Edge and Chrome, a preview Windows app, a customizable dashboard with asset statistics, runnable checklists, relationship maps, password health reports, per-client exports, encrypted backups with verified restore, a system status page, a read-only client portal, and Docker or Windows hosting. Not yet included: a signed Windows app installer, and IT Glue or ITBoost importers.
